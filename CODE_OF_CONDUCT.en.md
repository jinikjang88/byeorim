# Code of Conduct

> Korean original: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). If this translation falls out of sync, the Korean version is canonical.

Byeorim is a place where non-technical founders, new-domain developers, and senior engineers meet. People from such different backgrounds can only work in the same codebase if there is an explicit promise about how to treat each other. This document is that promise.

This code is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and shaped to fit Byeorim's tone.

## Our pledge

Every Byeorim participant pledges the following.

We treat each other with respect, regardless of age, body, disability, nationality, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, economic background, or technical level. A cafe owner touching code for the first time and a senior engineer with ten years of experience should both feel safe asking a question in the same issue thread.

## Expected behavior

- Respect the perspectives and experiences of others
- Direct critique at code and decisions, not at people
- Acknowledge mistakes, apologize, and learn from them
- Prioritize what helps the whole community over personal gain
- Take "basic" questions from non-technical users seriously. They are Byeorim's priority-one user.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome physical contact described in any form
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (address, email, etc.) without explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibility

The project maintainer is responsible for enforcing this code. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, PRs, or any other contribution that violates the code, and will explain the reason when needed.

## Scope

This code applies to every Byeorim space: the GitHub repository, issue tracker, PR discussions, and any space where someone is officially representing Byeorim.

## Reporting violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code, report it through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jinikjang88/byeorim/issues). Every report is reviewed and answered promptly.

If the report involves another participant or contributor and you cannot file it as a public issue, write "Private review requested" in the body. The maintainer will follow up through a private channel.

## Enforcement guidelines

When responding to a violation, the maintainer follows the steps below.

### 1. Correction

Impact: Use of inappropriate language, or unprofessional or unwelcome conduct.

Consequence: A private message explaining what was inappropriate and why. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

Impact: A single incident or a pattern of behavior that violates the code.

Consequence: No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the code, for a specified period. Time is given for the violator to review the code. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

Impact: A serious violation, including continued inappropriate behavior.

Consequence: A temporary ban from any kind of public interaction with the community. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

Impact: Demonstrating a pattern of inappropriate behavior, including harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of a group.

Consequence: A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
